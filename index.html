FAQ

What is Canton Network?

A privacy-enabled public blockchain built for regulated financial institutions, supporting atomic settlement of tokenized assets.

Which wallets are supported?

EVM wallets (MetaMask, etc.) for source assets and Canton-compatible wallets for receiving USDX & CC.

Is the swap private?

Yes. Transactions on Canton benefit from sub-transaction level privacy — only involved parties can see details.

What are the fees?

0.3% swap fee + network fees. Competitive rates for institutional and retail users.
